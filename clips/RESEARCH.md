# How clipping became a $1M+ business (research, Nov 17, 2026)

Almost all revenue and payout figures are **self-reported** by the companies to reporters. None are audited. Vendor-blog figures are marked [unverified].

## Who made the money: the brokers
The $1M+ businesses **sell views to creators and brands at a CPM (price per 1,000 views), then pay a crowd of clippers less**. Individual clippers mostly stay small: the median side-hustler makes about $200 a month ([Moneywise/Bankrate](https://moneywise.com/managing-money/how-to-earn-money/side-hustle-clipping-social-media-risks)).

## The platforms
- **Whop** as a company: ~$400M annualized revenue, $5.1B valuation [Sacra estimate] ([Sacra](https://sacra.com/research/whop)). Content Rewards had paid **$2.58M to 8,466 earners** by early 2026, per Whop's own blog ([Content Rewards](https://contentrewards.com/blog/get-paid-clip-streamers)): **about $305 per earner**. Takes 10%, settles in about 10 days, permanently bans view-botting ([ClipSpeed](https://www.clipspeed.ai/blog/whop-content-rewards-clipping-guide.html)).
- **Vyro** (MrBeast/ViewStats, Oct 2025): ~$3 per 1K views, capped at $1,000 per post ([Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2025/10/14/mrbeast-vyro-clipping-platform-viewstats-expansion/)).
- Brand rates seen: $0.50 per 1K (Polymarket), $1 per 1K (MLB), $25 per 1K (an AI startup) ([NPR via Michigan Public](https://www.michiganpublic.org/2026-05-12/the-clipping-economy-how-short-form-video-clippers-are-overrunning-the-internet)).

## Streamers paying clippers directly
- **N3on paid $1.4M to 303 clippers in 5 weeks** at $40–50 per 100K views; Kick pays part of his ~1,000-clipper network ([Business Insider via Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/04/29/n3on-spending-millions-stream-clippers-tiktok-kick/)).
- Over 30 days (Aug 2026), **1,820 accounts** posted clips of six top streamers. IShowSpeed and Kai Cenat drew 97% of clip views ([CreatorDB](https://creatordb.app/creator-news/the-rise-of-the-clip-economy/)). The field is crowded; volume and speed win.

## What works
- **Volume over polish:** "They let the algorithms… figure out the quality" (Twitch CEO, [TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/media-platforms/tv/creators-rise-of-clipping-explained/)).
- **Combative, high-emotion moments** perform best.
- **Post within hours** of the stream.
- **A hook in the first 1–2 seconds**, plus captions.
- **Clipper → agency:** free proof campaigns → case studies with view counts → cold DMs → referrals.

## What kills accounts (important for Instagram/Facebook)
- **Meta, July 2025:** accounts that "repeatedly reuse someone else's videos" lose Facebook monetization and get reduced distribution on everything they post ([Meta](https://creators.facebook.com/blog/combating-unoriginal-content/), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/07/14/following-youtube-meta-announces-crackdown-on-unoriginal-facebook-content)). A March 2026 update says minor edits don't count as original ([exchange4media](https://www.exchange4media.com/digital-news/meta-updates-policies-to-reward-original-creators-and-limit-reposted-content-152931.html)).
- **Instagram, April 30, 2026:** accounts that mostly repost untransformed content are **no longer recommended to non-followers** ([Tubefilter](https://tubefilter.com/2026/04/30/instagram-removes-algorithm-recommendations-repost-content-aggregator)).
- **Whop:** permanent bans for botting; fraud losses can be taken from your earnings.
- **Budgets run dry:** first come, first paid.
- **Clips with music in them:** their ad revenue goes to the rights holder.

## What this means for Clipamole
1. **Facebook Content Monetization is not a realistic income source** for clips of other people unless the creator authorizes us (an official or collab page). Facebook is reach only, and it's at risk of being suppressed.
2. **Instagram pays through Whop campaigns, not Instagram itself.** Our clips must be visibly transformed (hook, captions, our layout, our framing), or the account stops being recommended.
3. **The million-dollar path is becoming a broker.** Steps 1–3 in `clips/PLAN.md`: earn from campaigns, collect proof, then sell CPM campaigns to streamers and small brands and pay other clippers through Whop.
