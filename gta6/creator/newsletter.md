# {BRAND} Newsletter (beehiiv)

**Plan:** beehiiv Free is free up to 2,500 subscribers with unlimited sends. A single **Welcome Email** works on Free (Settings → Welcome Email). Multi-step automations need a paid plan, so don't build drips yet.
**Before the first send:** set the publication name to `{BRAND}` (no GTA, Rockstar, Vice City or Leonida), upload an original logo, and enter the **physical mailing address** beehiiv puts in the footer. US anti-spam law (CAN-SPAM) requires a valid postal address; a PO box works. Turn on double opt-in if spam signups appear.
**Signup points:** the tracker footer and the "Saved!" toast, video descriptions, #welcome on Discord, and pinned YouTube comments.
**Cadence:** weekly, Sundays at ~10 a.m. ET, starting **Sun Nov 22**.
