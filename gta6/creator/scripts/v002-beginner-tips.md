## How the owner uses this script
1. **During your first sessions**, keep a running note on your phone: every time you think "oh, I should have known that", write one line. That list becomes the tips.
2. Capture one clip **showing** each tip (the shopping list below gives the likely candidates; swap in your own).
3. Read the script with your 10 best tips filled in, 2-second pause between `##` sections. **Only include tips you verified yourself.** If a candidate below isn't true in the game, drop it.
4. Upload clips + `v002-vo.m4a`.
