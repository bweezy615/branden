## Rules for every upload (read once, apply always)

- **Own footage, own voice.** Record on your console and narrate with your USB mic. No AI voiceover, no AI-generated footage, no reused compilations (YouTube's inauthentic-content policy). AI may help draft an outline; you rewrite it and say it in your own words.

- **No spoilers.** No story beats in titles or thumbnails. No ending or cutscene compilations. Skip mission cutscenes when capturing, and blur mission names that give away the plot.
- **No cheats, glitches for money, money drops, modded or "recovered" accounts, boosting or account sales.** Don't show them, explain them or link to them.
- **Brand:** never put "GTA", "Grand Theft Auto", "Rockstar", "Vice City" or "Leonida" in the channel name, handle, logo or banner. Saying "GTA VI" in a title or description to describe the video is fine.
- **Thumbnails:** use your own captures (after launch) or your own photos and text (before launch). No official key art, logo or character renders.
- **Trailer clips (pre-launch only):** if you use any, keep them to a few seconds, talk over them and credit "Footage: Rockstar Games". Expect a Content ID claim, which can send that video's ad revenue to the rights holder. Your own graphics are safer.
- **Upload settings:** Made for kids = **No** (the game is rated ESRB M). Altered/synthetic content = **No**. Add each video to a playlist ("Launch Week", "Where to Find", "Vehicles").
- **Say the disclosure out loud** in any video where you mention gear: "Links in the description are affiliate links. I earn a small commission at no extra cost to you."
- **Capture pipeline:** test it on Video 1 today: console capture, then transfer (USB stick or the companion app), then the free editor, then export at 1080p. One user reports PS5 transfers arrive in 10–17 minute chunks, so record in short segments.
