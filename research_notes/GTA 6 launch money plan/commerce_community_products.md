## 1. Merch / print-on-demand: how platforms treat GTA/Rockstar IP, safe angles, and what sells

### Takeaway
Rockstar/GTA marks and art are a takedown risk on every POD platform: Redbubble removes on complaint *or proactively*, and repeat infringement can mean permanent account disablement. The viable lane is original "Vice City / Miami / Florida-man" aesthetic designs with no Rockstar marks, logos, character likenesses, or the "GTA"/"Grand Theft Auto" names. Demand clearly exists (Etsy and Redbubble already have large GTA-tagged inventories), but much of that existing inventory is infringing and lives on borrowed time.

### Cited Findings
- Redbubble requires consent before using someone else's picture, logo, trademark, copyright or publicity right; it runs a notice-and-takedown process **and** may take works down proactively without a specific report if it becomes aware of potential infringement — [Redbubble IP/Publicity Rights Policy](https://help.redbubble.com/hc/en-us/articles/201579195-Redbubble-IP-Publicity-Rights-Policy)
- Redbubble: accounts violating the IP policy are subject to its repeat-infringer policy "up to and including immediate and permanent disablement of the account" — [Redbubble IP Policy](https://help.redbubble.com/hc/en-us/articles/201579195-Redbubble-IP-Publicity-Rights-Policy)
- Redbubble on parody/fair use: it "might" apply in limited circumstances, is "not the same as freedom of speech," differs by country, and Redbubble advises consulting an attorney; it still must act on takedown reports regardless — [Redbubble IP Policy](https://help.redbubble.com/hc/en-us/articles/201579195-Redbubble-IP-Publicity-Rights-Policy)
- Redbubble operates a licensed "Fan Art Program" (partner programme), the only sanctioned route for fan art of participating brands; no evidence found that Rockstar/Take-Two participates — [Redbubble IP Policy nav](https://help.redbubble.com/hc/en-us/articles/201579195-Redbubble-IP-Publicity-Rights-Policy); [Facebook Redbubble group (anecdote)](https://www.facebook.com/groups/theredbubble/posts/1330136660871213/)
- Redbubble currently lists many "Rockstar Games" wall-art results (multiple pages) — evidence of demand and of infringing listings persisting — [Redbubble search](https://www.redbubble.com/shop/rockstar+games+prints?country=US&deviceType=mobile&enableRedirects=true&locale=en&page=3&pageSize=82)
- Etsy has an active "GTA 6 artwork" market category — [Etsy market](https://www.etsy.com/market/gta_6_artwork)
- Take-Two is an active IP enforcer: it sued PlayerAuctions in 2025 alleging copyright and trademark infringement, and an LA federal judge allowed "the bulk" of those claims to proceed on Aug 27, 2025 — [MSK/Law360](https://www.msk.com/newsroom-news-2924); [Loeb & Loeb](https://www.loeb.com/en/insights/publications/2025/09/take-two-interactive-software-inc-v-playerauctions-inc)

### Inferences
- Rockstar's launch window is when brand-protection vendors sweep marketplaces hardest; selling anything with "GTA," "Grand Theft Auto," "Rockstar," the VI logo, Lucia/Jason likenesses, or traced key art is the highest-risk merch play and can cost the whole shop (not just a listing).
- Lower-risk angles: original retro-synthwave/Miami-Vice-era palettes, "Florida Man"/"Leonida-ish" Florida humor without the fictional place names tied to marks, generic "gamer" launch-week humor ("Took PTO for a video game," "Do not disturb: I'm in Florida (digitally)"), neon palm/sunset typography. Note "Vice City" is a Rockstar-associated term; using it as a product title/tag is a gray area — keep it out of designs and titles to be safe (inference; no platform-specific ruling found).
- POD startup cost is near-zero (no inventory); margin per shirt is thin, and evergreen-ness matters more than launch spikes.

### Gaps
- Did not retrieve Etsy's, Printful/Printify's, or Amazon Merch on Demand's IP policy pages directly; Amazon Merch is known to be strict on trademarked terms but I have no 2026 citation.
- No reliable sales data found for GTA-adjacent non-infringing designs (no case studies with revenue numbers).
- No evidence found on whether Rockstar has filed bulk takedowns on Redbubble/Etsy in 2026 specifically.
- Whether "Vice City" or "Leonida" are registered US trademarks was not checked (USPTO search would resolve this).

## 2. Affiliate marketing: Amazon rates, pre-order programs, key resellers, gear

### Takeaway
Amazon pays almost nothing on the GTA-adjacent big-ticket items (1% on physical games/consoles, 2% on TVs and digital games), so the money is in accessories (headsets 3%, most other gear 4%, PC components 2.5%). Best Buy ran a dedicated GTA 6 physical pre-order affiliate campaign at 5% (May 2026) — far better than Amazon, but pre-orders are largely past. Key resellers like Eneba/Loaded (CDKeys) carry legitimacy and revocation risk; Green Man Gaming is an authorized retailer but GTA VI is console-only at launch, which limits PC-key affiliate value.

### Cited Findings
- Amazon Associates standard (offsite) fixed rates, checked live 2026-11-09: Physical Video Games & Video Game Consoles **1.00%**; Digital Video Games **2.00%**; Televisions **2.00%**; PC / PC Components **2.50%**; Headphones **3.00%**; all other categories **4.00%** — [Amazon Associates Standard Commission Income Statement](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GRXPHT8U84RAYDXZ)
- Corroborated by Lasso (checked 14 Oct 2026): 2% TVs/Digital Video Games, 1% Physical Video Games & Consoles; Amazon Games (Amazon-published titles) pays 20% but is not on the standard card; Associates pay Net-60 — [Lasso](https://getlasso.co/amazon-affiliate-commission-rate/)
- Amazon's separate "Onsite" commission table lists 0.50% for Physical Video Games & Consoles, TVs and Digital Video Games for onsite qualifying purchases (and 1.00% for TVs/Digital Video Games under Creator Ads) — [Amazon Onsite Commission Income](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/G4ARBJC7Z2NK48CA)
- Best Buy affiliate email campaign "GTA 6 Pre Order (Physical Game)" ran May 18–21, 2026 and offered affiliates **5% commission** on physical copy sales; emails were traced to a legitimate affiliate platform — [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/GTA-6-pre-order-leak-is-very-real-e-mails-traced-to-legitimate-affiliate-platform-as-May-18-date-firms-up.1296959.0.html); [Complex](https://www.complex.com/pop-culture/a/bernadette-giacomazzo/gta-6-best-buy-preorder-leak)
  - Possible conflict: an aggregator says Rockstar's pre-order announcement was June 24, 2026 — [GTA6Hype](https://www.gta6hype.com/blog/gta6-creator-monetization-revolution/). The Best Buy campaign may have been scheduled before the official go-live; unclear.
- Eneba runs a GTA 6 pre-order hub (PlayStation and Xbox) with a "HUB15" 15% discount code — [Eneba](https://www.eneba.com/hub/deals/preorder-gta-6/)
- Key reseller legitimacy (June 2026, updated Nov 4, 2026): Green Man Gaming is an authorized retailer with direct publisher contracts (lowest revocation risk); Eneba is a hybrid marketplace with third-party sellers (most variable revocation risk; Sept 2026 Trustpilot complaints of invalid codes and post-checkout fees); Loaded (formerly CDKeys) buys keys in bulk via regional/promotional channels (middle risk); revocation can happen months after purchase — [Tech Insider](https://tech-insider.org/eneba-vs-cdkeys-vs-green-man-gaming-2026/)

### Inferences
- Example math: a $500 console bundle via Amazon earns ~$5; a $60 game earns $0.60; a $150 headset at 3% earns $4.50. Affiliate income only matters at high traffic — and without video or a website, the traffic must come from Discord, newsletter, Reddit (where affiliate links are often banned), or social posts.
- "Launch-week setup" gear lists (headset, controller, TV with low input lag, capture card, external SSD for PS5/Xbox) are the best Amazon play; the game itself is near-worthless as an affiliate item.
- Promoting grey-market key sellers for a console-only launch carries reputational risk (invalid/revoked codes land on the recommender's audience) and adds little, since no PC key exists at launch.

### Gaps
- Could not confirm current affiliate commission rates for GameStop, Green Man Gaming, Instant Gaming, Humble, Eneba, or Loaded (none found in reliable sources).
- No data on Best Buy's standard (non-campaign) video-game affiliate rate in 2026.
- No data on whether PS Store/Xbox Store digital codes are affiliate-eligible via any US retailer.

## 3. Digital products (guides, trackers, overlays, thumbnails, wallpapers)

### Takeaway
Etsy is already flooded with cheap "GTA VI / Vice City" stream-overlay packs ($2–$16), which shows demand but also commoditization and heavy IP exposure (names and aesthetics tied to the game). Original, non-branded "neon/retro-Miami" overlay packs, generic streamer kits, and game-agnostic trackers are the defensible version; Rockstar art, logos, map rips, or screenshots in paid products are infringement risks.

### Cited Findings
- Etsy listing "Neon Vice (GTA VI) Stream Package (Static) | Twitch Overlays & Banners" priced **$2.38** — [Etsy GTA 6 overlay](https://www.etsy.com/market/gta_6_overlay)
- Etsy "GTA 6 Twitch Stream Overlay Package" at **$9.93** and a Vice City neon pink overlay pack — [Etsy Twitch GTA](https://www.etsy.com/market/twitch_gta); another GTA 6 overlay package listed at **$16.49** (discounted from $32.98) — [Etsy GTA 6 stream](https://www.etsy.com/market/gta_6_stream)
- Independent sellers (StreamSpell, byKron) market "GTA VI-inspired"/"Six Vibes" overlay packages for Twitch/OBS/Streamlabs — [StreamSpell](https://www.streamspell.com/post/best-gta-vi-stream-overlays-for-streamlabs); [byKron](https://bykron.com/products/gta-vi-six-vibes-stream-overlay)
- Redbubble/Etsy-style IP rules (consent required for logos/trademarks; proactive removals; account termination for repeat infringers) apply equally to digital downloads — [Redbubble IP Policy](https://help.redbubble.com/hc/en-us/articles/201579195-Redbubble-IP-Publicity-Rights-Policy)

### Inferences
- Price compression ($2–$16) means overlay packs are a volume game; a newcomer two weeks before launch is late unless differentiated (e.g., animated, Streamer.bot/OBS-ready, multi-platform including Kick).
- Text-only guides/checklists (100% completion trackers, collectibles checklists, money-making route spreadsheets) are copyright-safer if written from scratch without Rockstar's maps/art, but will compete with free wikis and IGN/GameFAQs within days of launch; their window is short.
- A "launch-night streamer kit" not tied to GTA (generic synthwave theme) can be sold all year and pinned to GTA only via marketing copy (nominative mention), which is lower risk than using "GTA" in the product name.

- No evidence of Rockstar takedowns targeting Etsy overlay sellers specifically.

## 4. Paid communities and newsletters (Discord, Patreon, Whop, beehiiv/Substack)

### Takeaway
Discord Server Subscriptions are US-only and net ~84.6% after processing and the 10% platform cut; Patreon now takes 10% (new creators since Aug 2025) plus card fees; beehiiv charges a flat plan fee (0% cut) and offers an ad network. Paid conversion is low (beehiiv median 0.62% of subscribers pay), so a launch-driven community needs thousands of free members before paid tiers matter.

### Cited Findings
- Discord Server Subscriptions: 90/10 split; must be 18+ — [Discord Creator Revenue FAQ](https://creator-support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/10424143128343-Creator-Revenue-FAQ); [Discord blog](https://discord.com/blog/server-and-creator-subscriptions)
- 2026 details (Oct 22, 2026): US-based owners with US banking/ID only; 10% platform fee applies *after* a 6% desktop/browser processing fee, so $1,000 collected yields **~$846**; iOS buyers pay higher prices; subscriptions cannot be bought in the Android app; first payout needs $100 balance, then $25; paid monthly — [Built by Foundry](https://www.builtbyfoundry.io/blog/discord-server-subscriptions-2026-846-from-1000)
- Discord tiers reportedly priced $2.99–$199.99/month, up to three tiers (social post, secondary) — [Instagram post](https://www.instagram.com/p/DeIWN-0Gl2f/?hl=hr)
- Patreon: 10% for creators who started after Aug 4, 2025, plus 2.9% + $0.30 card processing; beehiiv: free up to 2,500 subscribers without paid subs, paid plans ~$49–$59/month, 0% cut on paid subscriptions, plus Stripe fees; beehiiv has a built-in ad network and referral tools — [Everything-PR comparison](https://everything-pr.com/substack-patreon-beehiiv-comparison)
- beehiiv raised prices in 2026 (e.g., 2,500 subscribers: $61 → $69 on the new "Lite" tier) — [EmailOctopus](https://emailoctopus.com/blog/beehiiv-price-increase-2026)
- beehiiv's median paid conversion rate is **0.62%** (≈6 paying per 1,000 subscribers) — [beehiiv State of Paid Newsletters 2026](https://www.beehiiv.com/blog/the-state-of-paid-newsletters-2026)
- beehiiv Ad Network lets newsletters earn by running placed ads — [beehiiv Ad Network FAQ](https://www.beehiiv.com/support/article/17507491038231-ad-network-faq)

### Inferences
- Example: 5,000-member Discord at 0.6–1% paid conversion × $5/month ≈ $150–$250/month gross, ~$130–$210 net. Real but small unless the community has a unique hook (crew-finding, completion races, deal alerts, RP-server hub later).
- Because GTA VI appears to launch single-player only, "crew/heist LFG" demand that drove GTA Online Discords may be muted at launch; communities based on guides, news (PC port, online mode announcements), and deal alerts fit better.
- A free newsletter monetized by beehiiv ads + Amazon gear links is the lowest-cost recurring play; a paid tier is optional.

### Gaps
- Whop fee structure for 2026 not found.
- No verified revenue case studies of GTA-specific Discord servers or gaming newsletters.
- No data quantifying how much Discord servers grow around big game launches.

## 5. Roleplay servers (FiveM today, GTA 6 RP outlook)

### Takeaway
FiveM is now Rockstar-owned (Cfx.re acquired Aug 2023) and monetization is allowed but tightly scoped: Tebex is the only permitted payment route, selling in-game currency for real money and chance-based mechanics are banned, and Rockstar marks cannot be used in server branding (rules rewritten Jan 12, 2026). Rockstar launched an official Cfx Marketplace (Jan 12, 2026) for scripts/vehicles/maps — selling resources there may suit a side project better than running a server. GTA VI RP is not possible at launch (console-only, single-player, no PC), so RP on GTA VI is a 2027+ speculation.

### Cited Findings
- Rockstar acquired Cfx.re (FiveM/RedM) and announced it Aug 11, 2023, expanding its mod policy to include roleplay creators — [Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/rockstar-acquires-community-roleplay-team-cfx-re); [GTA6Hype quoting Newswire](https://www.gta6hype.com/blog/gta6-creator-monetization-revolution/)
- FiveM rules were rewritten in a Creator Platform License Agreement dated Jan 12, 2026 — [Cfx.re PLA PDF](https://static.cfx.re/platform-license-agreement-12-jan-2026.pdf); [GameServerKings summary](https://www.gameserverkings.com/knowledge-base/fivem/server-monetisation/)
- Cfx docs: "Tebex is our authorized monetization partner"; "The use of any other platform or payment provider is prohibited and is a violation of the Platform License Agreement" (PayPal, Patreon, Ko-fi, etc. not allowed) — [GameServerKings quoting docs.fivem.net](https://www.gameserverkings.com/knowledge-base/fivem/server-monetisation/); [Cfx forum](https://forum.cfx.re/t/fivem-server-monetization/5278213)
- Selling in-game virtual currency for real money is prohibited; chance-based mechanics (loot boxes) banned even for in-game currency; storefronts must identify the operator and include a Rockstar disclaimer — [GameServerKings](https://www.gameserverkings.com/knowledge-base/fivem/server-monetisation/)
- PLA: you may not use Rockstar logos/trademarks/IP in any way implying endorsement, nor as part of a Custom Server's name or branding — [Cfx.re PLA PDF](https://static.cfx.re/platform-license-agreement-12-jan-2026.pdf)
- Unsupported server artifacts older than three months become unjoinable from the server browser — [GameServerKings quoting Cfx.re](https://www.gameserverkings.com/knowledge-base/fivem/server-monetisation/)
- Tebex is merchant of record, handles chargebacks; NoPixel and Hypixel are listed customers — [Tebex](https://www.tebex.io/server). Older Cfx forum discussion references a 15% commission or $50/month Tebex plan (historical; current pricing not verified) — [Cfx forum (2020-era thread)](https://forum.cfx.re/t/announcing-tebex-partnership-server-monetization-commerce-2-0/1285323?page=6)
- Cfx Marketplace ("The Official Rockstar Modding UGC Marketplace for RedM & FiveM") launched Jan 12, 2026 with 16 creators; NoPixel, LB Phone and Tiger Scripts listed as coming soon; creators sell props, vehicles, maps and scripts to server owners — [GTA6Hype](https://www.gta6hype.com/blog/gta6-creator-monetization-revolution/) (aggregator, citing RockstarINTEL)
- Marketplace content cannot be resold/redistributed standalone outside a Custom Server; selling your own work via your own Tebex store is fine; escrowed resources fail with "You lack the required entitlement" if unlicensed — [GameServerKings](https://www.gameserverkings.com/knowledge-base/fivem/server-monetisation/)
- Unverified claims: a CNET Facebook post says Rockstar "officially partnered with NoPixel, ensuring GTA 6 will fully support roleplay servers" — [CNET Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/cnet/posts/rockstars-latest-gta-online-move-puts-gta-roleplay-nopixel-and-the-future-of-gta/1461244359198752/); a Reddit user mentions a "Rockstar backed NoPixel server" launched recently — [r/FiveM](https://www.reddit.com/r/FiveM/comments/1wss4d6/life_after_gta_6_is_there_a_future/). The "GTA 6 will fully support RP" phrasing is not corroborated by a Rockstar primary source I found.
- HipHopGamer (rumor, April 2026) claimed GTA 6 UGC will be monetized via a marketplace and "will produce millionaires" — [GTA6Hype citing PC Gamer/GamesRadar](https://www.gta6hype.com/blog/gta6-creator-monetization-revolution/) (rumor; not confirmed)

### Inferences
- Running a FiveM server is not passive: it needs hosting, developers, staff/moderation, and anti-cheat; most small servers don't break even. A side project fits better as a **resource/script seller** (Tebex store or Cfx Marketplace) than a server operator.
- GTA V/FiveM RP may see a player dip as attention moves to GTA VI on consoles in November 2026, then possibly recover since GTA VI has no PC or online RP path yet.
- Any GTA VI RP opportunity depends on an unannounced PC release and modding/online policy; planning around it now is speculative.

- Cfx Marketplace revenue split not found.

## 6. Services, plus risky/prohibited models (accounts, money drops, boosting, cheats) with legal precedents

### Takeaway
Freelance services (thumbnail design, short-form editing for GTA creators, Discord setup, overlay customization) are low-risk and can be sold immediately, but they are active income, not passive. Selling modded accounts, in-game money, boosting, or mod menus is the one area with a long, documented record of Take-Two lawsuits, settlements, asset freezes and even house raids. With GTA VI single-player and microtransaction-free at launch (per reports), there is also little legitimate demand for "money drop" products, until an online mode exists.

### Cited Findings
- Take-Two sued PlayerAuctions (2025) alleging the marketplace had "thousands of listings for unauthorized, infringing GTA V" accounts and currency; Take-Two said "PlayerAuctions refuses to stop because it continues to rake in illicit profits" — [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/rockstar-owner-take-two-sues-playerauctions-for-allegedly-selling-hacked-gta-online-accounts-and-currency); [Polygon, Mar 7, 2025](https://www.polygon.com/gaming/536315/take-two-gta-5-online-black-market-lawsuit/)
- Aug 27, 2025: an LA federal judge let the bulk of Take-Two's copyright and trademark claims against PlayerAuctions proceed — [MSK/Law360](https://www.msk.com/newsroom-news-2924); [Loeb & Loeb](https://www.loeb.com/en/insights/publications/2025/09/take-two-interactive-software-inc-v-playerauctions-inc)
- Mar 26, 2026: court declined to dismiss PlayerAuctions' DMCA §512(f) counterclaim (misrepresentation in takedown notices); the case is ongoing — [Eric Goldman, Technology & Marketing Law Blog](https://blog.ericgoldman.org/archives/2026/03/do-dmca-takedown-notices-need-to-expressly-refer-to-the-lack-of-fair-use-take-two-v-playerauctions.htm)
- Take-Two also sued a China-based modded account seller in California — [RockstarINTEL](https://rockstarintel.com/gta-online-cheater-crackdown-lawsuit/) (date not captured)
- 2019: Take-Two settled with Erik Cameron, creator of the "Infamous" GTA V cheat (undisclosed sum, permanent injunction); Christopher Pei also settled; five people in Australia had assets frozen — [GamesIndustry.biz, Mar 12, 2019](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/take-two-settles-copyright-case-with-another-gta-v-cheat-creator)
- Take-Two sought $150,000 in damages plus ~$70,000 in legal fees from the maker of the "Elusive" mod menu — [Digital Trends](https://www.digitaltrends.com/gaming/take-two-sues-gta-online-modder-for-150000/)
- Mar 2019: Take-Two filed a copyright suit against makers of the "Evolve" mod menu — [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/2019/3/21/18274336/grand-theft-auto-online-evolve-mod-menu-cheating-copyright-lawsuit)
- Australia (2018): Take-Two's mod-menu crackdown included a house raid — [ABC News (Australia)](https://www.abc.net.au/news/science/2018-11-08/grand-theft-auto-cheat-crackdown-house-raid/10472358)
- A $25 mod menu for GTA Online / Red Dead Online shut down after Take-Two legal threats — [GameSpot via Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/GameSpot/posts/a-popular-mod-menu-being-sold-for-25-for-use-in-gta-online-and-red-dead-online-i/10165885513960436/)
- Court proceedings figure: GTA Online makes almost $8 million a week (cited in an aggregator's summary) — [GTA6Hype](https://www.gta6hype.com/blog/gta6-creator-monetization-revolution/) — explains Take-Two's motivation to litigate
- Also relevant: FiveM's PLA bans selling in-game currency for real money — [GameServerKings](https://www.gameserverkings.com/knowledge-base/fivem/server-monetisation/)

### Inferences
- Risk ranking (highest to lowest): mod menus/cheats (federal copyright suits, injunctions, six-figure damages) > modded accounts/money drops/account sharing (ToS bans plus marketplace litigation) > boosting (ToS bans) > legitimate services (none).
- Legit service pricing was not researched in depth; marketplaces like Fiverr would set the range. Services suit someone who already has editing/design skills.

### Gaps
- No 2026 market-rate data for GTA-creator video editing, thumbnails, or Discord setup services.
- No 2026 court outcome (damages/settlement) for Take-Two v. PlayerAuctions; still pending as of the last source (Mar–Jul 2026).
- Final outcome of the Elusive and Evolve cases not confirmed in fetched sources.

## 7. Gig/physical angle: launch events, local tie-ins, reselling collector's editions and consoles

### Takeaway
Scalping the $400 Collector's Box shows headline eBay asks of $800–$1,700, but those were asks while Rockstar still had stock; standard copies reportedly flipped for only ~$90. That makes reselling a speculative, capital-tied play with platform and reputational risk. Events and viewing parties are plausible but undocumented.

### Cited Findings
- Scalpers listed Rockstar's **$400** Collector's Box for "upwards of $1,500" while it was still in stock at Rockstar — [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-scalpers-are-already-trying-to-resell-rockstars-usd400-collectors-box-for-upwards-of-usd1-500-even-though-its-still-in-stock/)
- eBay listings for the Vice City Collector's Box ranged from ~$800 to over $1,700 — [Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-collectors-box-is-already-being-scalped/)
- Some scalpers flipped pre-ordered **standard** editions for around $90 — [IGN via Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/ign/posts/listings-for-pricier-versions-of-gta-6-have-popped-up-on-ebay-and-some-scalpers-/1585589189889547/)
- eBay currently shows 25+ "GTA 6" listings (Nov 2026), mostly low-priced items ($7–$25) plus a few ~$200 listings — [eBay](https://www.ebay.com/shop/gta-6?_nkw=gta+6)

### Inferences
- Listing (ask) prices are not sold prices; eBay fees (~13%+) and shipping erode margins. If Rockstar restocks or physical supply is ample, post-launch prices may fall below MSRP + fees. Not passive and not recommended for a small side project unless collector stock is already in hand.
- Launch-night viewing parties/tournaments at local venues (bars, game cafés) can be monetized via ticketing/sponsorship, but GTA VI is single-player, which limits the "competitive event" format.
