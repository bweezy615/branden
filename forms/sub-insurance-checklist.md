# SUBCONTRACTOR COMPLIANCE CHECKLIST

> ## No COI = no jobsite access. No exceptions, no "we'll get it Monday."
>
> **Why this form exists:** at your annual workers' compensation audit, the carrier reviews
> every dollar you paid to subcontractors. **For any sub who cannot produce proof of their
> own workers' comp coverage, the carrier charges you premium as if that person were your
> employee** — a five-figure surprise bill arriving with no warning and no negotiation.
> Ten minutes of paperwork now prevents it. → [§5.6](../docs/05-staffing-and-crew.md#56-subcontractor-management)
