# Forms & Templates

Working templates for daily operations. **Have an attorney review the Work Authorization
and the subcontractor agreement before first use** — contract requirements are
state-specific, and the disclosures your state mandates (right to cancel, lien notice,
deposit limits) must be added. → [§8.1](../docs/08-risk-insurance-compliance.md#81-licensing--the-fill-in-the-blank-checklist)

| Form | When it's used | Non-negotiable rule |
|---|---|---|
| [work-authorization.md](work-authorization.md) | Every emergency call, on site, before touching anything | **No signature, no work.** |
| [change-order.md](change-order.md) | Any scope change on any project | **Signed and priced before the work happens.** |
| [daily-job-log.md](daily-job-log.md) | Every project, every day | Filled out by the crew lead before leaving site. |
| [subcontractor-agreement.md](subcontractor-agreement.md) | Every subcontractor, before first job | Signed + COI + license verified first. |
| [sub-insurance-checklist.md](sub-insurance-checklist.md) | Every subcontractor, annually | **No COI = no jobsite access.** |
| [emergency-call-sheet.md](emergency-call-sheet.md) | Every emergency dispatch | Carried in the truck; drives the documentation standard. |

**Keep printed copies in every vehicle.** Tablets die, cell service fails in basements, and
the one night you can't produce a Work Authorization is the night you needed it.
