# EMERGENCY CALL SHEET

**Keep printed copies in every response vehicle.** Tablets die and basements have no
signal. This sheet drives the intake, the dispatch, and the documentation standard.

---

### ⚠️ SAFETY SCREEN FIRST — if ANY of these, redirect and do not dispatch

utility from outside. Call us back once they've cleared it."*

can reach it safely. If not, get out and call the power company."*
☐ **Structure actively collapsing / audible cracking** → *"Get everyone out now. Call 911."*

## PART 3 — ON SITE

### ⚠️ BEFORE TOUCHING ANYTHING

☐ **Work Authorization signed** → [work-authorization.md](work-authorization.md)
☐ Photo: house front with address visible
☐ Photos: wide shots of every affected room, all four corners
☐ Photos: close-ups of all damage
☐ Photo: the source of the loss
☐ Moisture readings — **photograph the meter against the surface**
☐ **Photo: pre-existing damage we did NOT cause** *(this protects us)*

### SITE SAFETY CHECK

☐ Power isolated where needed  ☐ Structure assessed as safe to enter
☐ Standing water assessed for electrical hazard  ☐ Adequate lighting set
☐ PPE on  ☐ Fall protection if above 6 ft
☐ **Two-person minimum** on structural or water emergencies after dark
☐ Generator outdoors only — **never in a garage or enclosed space**

### DURING

☐ Progress photos of each mitigation step
☐ Every piece of equipment photographed in place
☐ **Any concealed condition discovered photographed** *(rot, mold, prior bad repair,
missing header)*
☐ Customer told what we're doing, in plain language, before we do it

### BEFORE LEAVING

☐ After-photos, same angles as the before-shots
☐ Photo of the completed tarp / board-up / shoring
☐ Photo of the signed Work Authorization
☐ Equipment logged with written pickup date: ____________
☐ Completion Record on the Work Authorization filled out
☐ Site secure  ☐ Customer walked through what was done  ☐ Site left clean
☐ Business card and branded folder left with the customer
