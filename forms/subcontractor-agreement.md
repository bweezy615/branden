## 1. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR STATUS

Subcontractor is an independent contractor, not an employee. Subcontractor:

- controls the means, methods, and details of performing the work;
- supplies their own tools, equipment, and materials except as expressly agreed;
- sets their own work hours consistent with the project schedule;
- may perform work for others;
- is solely responsible for their own taxes, insurance, and employee obligations;
- is responsible for hiring, supervising, paying, and terminating their own personnel.

> **Note for Contractor:** this clause does not make a worker independent — the actual
> working relationship does. If you direct their methods, set their hours, supply their
> tools, and they work only for you, they are an employee regardless of what this document
> says. → [§2.5](../docs/02-formation-and-ownership.md#25-the-worker-problem--read-this-first)

## 2. SCOPE OF WORK

Work is authorized **per project** by a written Work Order specifying scope, price,
schedule, and site. **No work is authorized without a signed Work Order.** Verbal
authorizations are not binding on Contractor.

Work Order #: ____________ *(attach to this Agreement for each project)*

## 3. INSURANCE — CONDITION PRECEDENT

**Subcontractor shall not enter any jobsite until all of the following are on file with
Contractor:**

| Coverage | Minimum limit |
|---|---|
| Commercial General Liability | $1,000,000 occurrence / $2,000,000 aggregate |
| **Workers' Compensation** | **Statutory** — or a valid state exemption acceptable to Contractor's carrier |
| Commercial Auto | $1,000,000 combined single limit |
| Employer's Liability | $500,000 |

- Contractor shall be named **additional insured** on the CGL policy, with a **waiver of
  subrogation** in Contractor's favor.
- Subcontractor shall provide **30 days' written notice** before cancellation or material
  change.
- Coverage shall be maintained continuously throughout the work and through the completed
  operations period.
- **Lapse of any required coverage is a material breach** and grounds for immediate
  termination and removal from the site.

→ Verify against [sub-insurance-checklist.md](sub-insurance-checklist.md) before first job
and at every renewal.

## 4. LICENSING & COMPLIANCE

Subcontractor warrants that it holds and will maintain all licenses, registrations,
certifications, and permits required for the work, including **EPA Lead-Safe RRP
certification** where work disturbs painted surfaces in pre-1978 structures. Subcontractor
shall comply with all applicable federal, state, and local laws, building codes, and OSHA
regulations.

## 5. PAYMENT

- Payment terms: **Net ______ days** from receipt of a correct invoice and satisfactory
  completion of the invoiced work.
- **Retainage:** ______% withheld on Work Orders over $5,000, released upon final
  acceptance and receipt of a final lien waiver.
- Invoices must reference the Work Order number and Contractor's job number.
- **Lien waivers required with every payment** — conditional waiver with progress payments,
  unconditional final waiver with final payment.
- Contractor may withhold payment for defective work, failure to provide lien waivers,
  lapsed insurance, or damage caused by Subcontractor, until cured.
- Payment by Contractor's customer is **not** a condition of payment to Subcontractor
  *(confirm your state's rule on "pay-if-paid" clauses with counsel)*.

## 6. SCHEDULE

Subcontractor shall perform in accordance with the schedule stated in the Work Order and
shall coordinate with other trades. Subcontractor shall notify Contractor **immediately**
of any anticipated delay. Subcontractor is responsible for costs caused by its own
unexcused delay, including other trades' standby time.

## 7. QUALITY & WARRANTY

- All work shall be performed in a workmanlike manner, per code and manufacturer
  specifications.
- Subcontractor warrants its work against defects for **one (1) year** from substantial
  completion, and passes through all manufacturer warranties.
- Warranty callbacks shall be responded to within **three (3) business days**.
- Defective work shall be corrected at Subcontractor's expense. If Subcontractor fails to
  correct within a reasonable time, Contractor may correct it and deduct the cost.

## 8. CHANGES

**No change in scope or price is valid without a written change order signed by
Contractor.** Subcontractor shall not act on scope changes requested directly by the
property owner — such requests shall be referred to Contractor immediately.

## 9. JOBSITE CONDUCT

Subcontractor and its personnel shall: maintain a clean and safe work area, remove debris
daily, wear required PPE, use fall protection above 6 feet, refrain from smoking on site,
refrain from playing music without the homeowner's permission, dress and behave
professionally, and protect the customer's property, landscaping, pets, and finishes.

**Zero tolerance for impairment on any jobsite.** Violation is grounds for immediate
removal and termination.

Subcontractor shall not solicit Contractor's customers for work outside this Agreement
during the term and for **______ months** thereafter.

## 10. SAFETY

Subcontractor is solely responsible for the safety of its own personnel and shall comply
with all OSHA standards and Contractor's site safety rules. Subcontractor shall report any
incident or near-miss to Contractor **the same day**.

## 11. INDEMNIFICATION

To the fullest extent permitted by law, Subcontractor shall indemnify, defend, and hold
harmless Contractor and the property owner from claims, damages, losses, and expenses
arising out of Subcontractor's performance, **to the extent caused by the negligent acts or
omissions of Subcontractor** or anyone for whose acts it is responsible.

> ⚠️ *Several states restrict or void broad indemnity clauses in construction contracts.
> Counsel must conform this section to your state's anti-indemnity statute.*

## 12. LIENS

Subcontractor shall keep the property free of liens arising from its work and shall
promptly discharge any lien filed by itself or its own suppliers or subcontractors.
Subcontractor shall provide lien waivers as required in Section 5.

## 13. TERMINATION

Contractor may terminate for convenience on ______ days' written notice, paying for work
satisfactorily completed to that date. Contractor may terminate **immediately for cause**,
including: lapsed insurance, lapsed license, defective work not cured, unexcused delay,
safety violation, impairment, or breach of jobsite conduct.

## 14. CONFIDENTIALITY

Subcontractor shall not disclose Contractor's pricing, customer information, or business
methods.

## 15. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

Disputes shall first be submitted to mediation. Unresolved disputes shall be settled by
binding arbitration in ______________________ under ____________________ rules. This
Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of ______________.

## 16. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

This Agreement, together with each signed Work Order, constitutes the entire agreement and
supersedes all prior discussions. Amendments must be in writing and signed by both parties.
If any provision is held unenforceable, the remainder stays in effect.
