**Prepared:** August 2026
**Seller:** Marvo
**Target account:** Budget Barn
**Engagement:** 30-day paid social test, Meta (Facebook + Instagram)

---

## The one-paragraph version

Budget Barn does not want to buy Meta ads. They want to know whether Meta ads work
for Budget Barn — and right now that question is costing them nothing to ignore and
everything to guess at. So we don't sell them advertising. We sell them **a
thirty-day, fixed-price answer to that question**, delivered with the media spend
already inside the price, a scoreboard we agree on before a dollar moves, and a
day-14 exit if the numbers say stop. One number, one invoice, one decision. The ad
spend is a cost of goods inside our package — the same way a restaurant doesn't
itemize the beef — and our margin lives in the spread between the package price and
what we actually put on the platform.

**Month one is not where the money is. Month one buys the right to bill month two
through twelve.** Everything in these documents serves that.

## The four numbers that decide whether this works

Everything else is commentary.

| # | Number | Target | Why it decides everything |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Gross margin per client-month** | ≥ 45% | Below this, the ad spend is eating the business |
| 2 | **Hours per client, month 2+** | ≤ 4 | Month 1 is ~11 hrs and barely pays. Renewals are the business. |
| 3 | **Month-2 renewal rate** | ≥ 50% | One-month clients are a treadmill. Renewals are compounding. |
| 4 | **Cash collected before launch** | 100% | Never float a client's ad spend. Not once. |

## Assumptions on the table

These are stated, not hidden. Correct them before the meeting and the numbers flex
cleanly.

1. ~~Budget Barn is a walk-in retail business.~~ **Resolved — it isn't.** Budget Barn
   TN is a **liquidation auction business** in Gallatin, TN, selling returns and
   overstock through weekly online auctions on HiBid, with local pickup and nationwide
   shipping. This changes the campaign structure and the success metric.
   **See [Section 6](06-budget-barn-account-brief.md).** Pricing and trust structure
   are unaffected.
2. **Marvo is the seller and the operator** — he pitches it and he runs it. If those
   are two people, the time cost in [Section 5](05-business-plan.md) splits but the
   totals hold.
3. **Budget Barn has never run real paid social** — maybe a boosted post or two.
   *Unconfirmed.* Check the Meta Ad Library before the meeting. If they've run and
   failed before, use the "burned before" objection track in
   [Section 1](01-the-pitch.md#objection-4).
4. **Ad spend is funded on Marvo's payment method**, client prepays the package. See
   [Section 2](02-pricing-and-money-mechanics.md#the-mechanics-how-the-money-actually-moves)
   for why, and the alternative if card float is a problem.
