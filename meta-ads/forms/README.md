# Client-Facing Forms

← [Back to Meta Ads index](../README.md)

Three documents. Use them in this order, every time, no exceptions.

| Order | Document | When | Why it exists |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [**Proposal**](budget-barn-proposal.md) | Hand over at the end of the pitch meeting | One page they can take home. Print it — never send a deck. |
| 2 | [**Scoreboard Agreement**](scoreboard-agreement.md) | Signed **before** any money is spent | Decides what "it worked" means while everyone is still calm. This is the document that prevents week-five arguments. |
| 3 | [**Service Agreement**](service-agreement.md) | Signed with payment, before launch | Payment terms, account ownership, the day-14 exit, and the no-guarantee clause. |

---

## Rules

- **Nothing runs until all three are signed and the money has cleared.** Not "we'll
  sort the paperwork after." That's how you end up arguing about results with no
  written definition of results.
- **Fill out the Scoreboard on a call or in person, never over email.** The conversation
  is worth more than the document — it's where you find out whether they can actually
  answer the phone when leads arrive.
- **Delete the italic internal notes before printing.** They're for Marco, not the
  client.
- Have a lawyer in your state review the Service Agreement once before you use it
  repeatedly. It's a template, not legal advice.

## Also worth keeping

Not client-facing, but keep a copy per engagement:

- Meta billing receipts, weekly — attach to every report
- A one-page final report per client — these become your case studies
- A dated note of what you promised verbally, if it went beyond these documents
