### Way 2 — Higher prices on inventory you already own

This is the one most people miss, and for an auction it may be the bigger half.

**You've already paid for the pallet. It costs you the same whether four people bid on
a lot or forty.** More bidders raise the final hammer price on the same goods — and
your 15% buyer's premium rises with it.

| | Example | **Your number** |
|---|---|---|
| Your current monthly hammer total | $9,000 | `$______` |
| A 5% lift from more competitive bidding | +$450 | `$______` |
| Plus 15% premium on that lift | +$68 | `$______` |
| **Value of a 5% price lift alone** | **~$518/month** | `$______` |

That's **more than a third of the whole cost**, from price competition alone, on
inventory that's already sitting in your warehouse.

### Way 3 — Bidders are repeat customers, not one-time sales

Someone who registers this month is still bidding next January.

| | Example | **Your number** |

**This is the honest way to read the month.** Month one might not pay for itself inside
