> owner will actually read it — that's a feature, and it's also why it's not exhaustive.

---

## 1. What Provider will do

Over a **30-day campaign period** beginning `___________`:

a) Build and launch advertising campaigns on Meta platforms (Facebook and Instagram)
   in an ad account owned by Client
b) Produce **`___` ad variations** and test **`___` audiences**
c) Purchase and place **$`_______` of Meta advertising media** on Client's behalf
d) Manage and optimize the campaigns daily for the full 30 days
e) Provide weekly written reports, a day-14 review meeting, and a final report
f) Attach Meta's platform billing receipts to each report

---

## 2. What Client will do

a) Create and retain ownership of a Meta Business Manager and ad account, and grant
   Provider partner-level administrative access
b) Supply photographs, video, offers, and business information needed for the ads
c) Respond to inquiries generated by the advertising within a reasonable time
d) Approve ad creative within 2 business days of receipt
e) Confirm that all materials supplied to Provider are Client's to use

---

## 3. Fee and payment

**Total fee: $`_______`, inclusive of all media spend.** Payable in full before the
campaign launches. No campaign will run before payment clears.

There are no additional charges. Client will receive no separate invoice from Meta for
the campaign media described in this agreement. Card processing fees, if any, are
absorbed by Provider.

---

## 4. Account ownership

**Client owns the Meta Business Manager, ad account, page, pixel, audience data, and
all campaign assets, from the first day and permanently.** Provider operates as a
granted partner and holds no ownership interest.

Client may revoke Provider's access at any time. On termination or expiry, Provider
will confirm removal of its access and its payment method within 3 business days, and
Client retains everything built.

**Provider will not request or accept Client's personal account login credentials.**

---

## 5. Day-14 termination right

At the day-14 review meeting, **Client may terminate this agreement for any reason.**

If Client terminates:
- All remaining unspent media is refunded within **5 business days**
- The management fee is **not refunded**, as that work is substantially complete at
  day 14 and Client retains the account and all assets built
- Provider will complete a clean handover at no additional charge

---

## 6. No guarantee of results

**Provider does not guarantee any level of sales, revenue, leads, inquiries, foot
traffic, engagement, or return on advertising spend.** Advertising performance depends
on market conditions, competition, the Client's offer, pricing, reputation, and the
Client's own handling of inquiries — none of which are under Provider's control.

Provider guarantees only: that the described work will be performed competently, that
the stated media budget will be spent on the Client's advertising, that platform
billing receipts will be provided, and that Provider will report results honestly
including when those results are poor.

Any performance figures discussed before or during this agreement — including targets
recorded in the Scoreboard Agreement — are **objectives, not promises.**

---

## 7. Platform risk

Meta may restrict, suspend, or disable advertising accounts, reject ads, or change
policies, pricing, or tooling, at its discretion and without notice. **Provider is not
liable for platform actions outside its control.**

If the account is disabled through no fault of Provider, Provider will work in good
faith to restore or rebuild delivery. If delivery cannot be restored within 10 days,
unspent media will be refunded.

If the account is restricted due to Client-supplied content or Client's business
practices, Provider is not obligated to refund the management fee.

---

## 8. Term and renewal

This agreement covers **30 days only.** It ends automatically on `___________`.

**There is no auto-renewal, no recurring charge, and no ongoing obligation on either
party.** Any continuation requires a new written agreement.

---

## 9. Confidentiality

Each party will keep the other's non-public business information confidential.
Provider may reference the engagement and anonymized results in marketing materials
unless Client checks here: ☐ **Client requests no public reference.**

---

## 10. Limitation of liability

Provider's total liability under this agreement is limited to the total fee paid by
Client. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages.
