## What this is

Thirty days of Facebook and Instagram advertising for Budget Barn, built and managed
start to finish, aimed at **getting more people registered and bidding in your weekly
auctions** — so you can find out whether paid social actually works for you instead of
wondering about it for another two years.

**I'm not going to promise you sales.** Nobody honestly can. What I'm promising is a

## Why I think your business fits this unusually well

**You have a real deadline every week.** Most businesses I work with have to invent a
reason for people to act now. Your lots close Monday at five. That's genuine urgency,
it renews every auction, and it never gets stale.

**More bidders raise your prices, not just your volume.** You've already paid for the
pallet — it costs you the same whether four people bid or forty. More bidders means the
same box sells for more, and your 15% premium goes up with it. That's on top of selling
more lots.

**A bidder isn't a sale, it's a subscriber.** Someone who registers this month is still
bidding next January.

**Your content is already the best-performing kind on the platform.** Pallet unboxings
and warehouse walk-throughs outperform polished, produced ads — and you generate that
footage every week just doing your job.
